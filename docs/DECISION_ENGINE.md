# Atlas Decision Engine (Layer 5) Architecture

## 1. Executive Summary

The Atlas Decision Engine represents Layer 5 of the Atlas quantitative infrastructure. It is a mathematically pure, deterministic, and highly explainable composition layer that transforms raw quantitative feature arrays (Layers 1-4) into directional recommendations, risk parameters, and interpretable evidence.

**CRITICAL ARCHITECTURAL RULE:** The Decision Engine NEVER writes to Atlas Core. It consumes Atlas features through the exact same read-only public API (`compute_features`) that any external system would use. It never invents features, and it never modifies the Registry. Everything it produces is traceable back to specific, immutable Atlas measurements.

## 3. Signal Extractors (The 22 Pillars)

The Decision Engine relies on exactly 22 signal extractors. Each extractor maps directly to specific `SIGNAL_FEATURE_IDS`, processes the raw values, and outputs a standardized dictionary containing `direction` (-1, 0, +1), `strength` (0.0 to 1.0), and `confidence` (0.0 to 1.0).

### 3.1 Directional & Trend Signals
1. **Trend (Weight: 0.09)**
   - **Reads:** `price.regime.above_sma_200`, `price.regime.ma50_above_ma200`, `price.motion.lr_slope_50`, `price.geometry.lr_r2_50`
   - **Computes:** Baseline trend alignment and linearity.

2. **Momentum (Weight: 0.07)**
   - **Reads:** `price.motion.log_return_10`, `price.motion.log_return_20`, `price.motion.ema_diff_5_20`
   - **Computes:** Velocity and short-term price dynamics.

3. **TrendMomentum (Weight: 0.03)**
   - **Reads:** `momentum_of_trend.motion.*`
   - **Computes:** The second derivative of price action; momentum of the trend itself.

4. **TrendAlignment (Weight: 0.05)**
   - **Reads:** `trend_relationships.regime.directional_consensus_score_20`, `trend_relationships.geometry.ema_cascade_score_10_20_50_100`
   - **Computes:** Multi-timeframe trend consistency and EMA cascade perfection.

### 3.2 Structure & Geometry Signals
5. **FractalCoherence (Weight: 0.03)**
   - **Reads:** `fractal_relationships.regime.self_similarity_score`, `fractal_relationships.statistics.multifractal_spread_h1_minus_h3_2_32`
   - **Computes:** Direction-neutral confidence modifier based on multi-scale geometric self-similarity.

6. **RelativeStrength (Weight: 0.03)**
   - **Reads:** `relative_strength.magnitude.rs_20`, `relative_strength.statistics.corr_50`
   - **Computes:** Outperformance vs. peer/benchmark (requires DEC-007 multi-symbol context).

7. **Regime (Weight: 0.08)**
   - **Reads:** `price.regime.chande_momentum_14`, `price.regime.choppiness_14`, `price.regime.adx_14`
   - **Computes:** Classical regime classification (trending vs. ranging).

8. **Structure (Weight: 0.02)**
   - **Reads:** `price.geometry.straightness_50`, `price.position.donchian_50`
   - **Computes:** Geometric straightness and channel breakout validity.

9. **MeanReversion (Weight: 0.06)**
   - **Reads:** `price.statistics.zscore_20`, `price.position.bb_percent_b_20_k2`, `price.position.fisher_20`
   - **Computes:** Extreme extension exhaustion and pullback probability.

### 3.3 Microstructure & Volume Signals
10. **Volume (Weight: 0.09)**
    - **Reads:** `volume.regime.pv_confirmation_20`, `volume.relative.price_vs_vwap_20`
    - **Computes:** Price-volume divergence and institutional participation footprints.

11. **Volatility (Weight: 0.02)**
    - **Reads:** `volatility.regime.tercile_200`, `volatility.expansion.short_long_ratio`
    - **Computes:** Volatility regime shifts, often a direction-neutral penalty to confidence if elevated.

12. **Liquidity (Weight: 0.03)**
    - **Reads:** `liquidity.magnitude.turnover_20`, `liquidity.regime.kyle_above_ma_50`
    - **Computes:** Market depth, execution capacity, and Kyle's lambda impact.

13. **Microstructure (Weight: 0.07)**
    - **Reads:** `microstructure.expansion.execution_imbalance_20`, `microstructure.geometry.wick_difference_ratio`
    - **Computes:** L1 order-flow imbalance proxies and high-participation wick rejections.

14. **MarketStructure (Weight: 0.12) - Highest Weight**
    - **Reads:** `market_structure.motion.bos_k5`, `market_structure.regime.choch_flag_k5`
    - **Computes:** L2 structural breaks (Break of Structure, Change of Character).

15. **Entropy (Weight: 0.01)**
    - **Reads:** `price.entropy.perm_o3_w30`, `price.entropy.spectral_60`
    - **Computes:** Market noise and randomness; directly penalizes voter confidence.

### 3.4 L3 State Machine Signals
16. **TrendState (Weight: 0.04)**
    - **Reads:** `trend_state.statistics.active_state_confidence`
    - **Computes:** L3 pure trend state extraction.

17. **RangeState (Weight: 0.03)**
    - **Reads:** `range_state.statistics.active_state_confidence`
    - **Computes:** Direction-neutral state (ranging vs breakout environment).

18. **ParticipationState (Weight: 0.03)**
    - **Reads:** `participation_state.statistics.active_state_confidence`
    - **Computes:** Accumulation (+1) vs. Distribution (-1).

19. **LiquidityState (Weight: 0.03)**
    - **Reads:** `liquidity_state.statistics.active_state_confidence`
    - **Computes:** Institutional liquidity vacuum or density states.

20. **RegimeState (Weight: 0.02)**
    - **Reads:** `market_regime.statistics.active_state_confidence`
    - **Computes:** L3 composite regime (trending_quiet, trending_volatile, etc).

### 3.5 L4 Derived Signals
21. **CrossFlow (Weight: 0.03)**
    - **Reads:** `cross_module_interactions.motion.trend_x_volume_signed_20`
    - **Computes:** Interactions between non-linear L2 modules (e.g. trend vs volume).

22. **MetaConfidence (Weight: 0.02)**
    - **Reads:** `meta_features.regime.l3_family_agreement_score`
    - **Computes:** Confidence derived from the cross-agreement of all L3 state machines.

## 4. The Voter System

The Voter module (`voter.py`) implements a strictly weighted linear aggregation of the 22 signal extractors. 

### 4.1 Weight Aggregation
Each signal $i$ contributes a score $S_i \in [-1, 1]$ and has a weight $W_i$. The raw directional score is:
$$ Score = \frac{\sum (S_i \times W_i)}{\sum W_i} $$
The weights always sum to exactly 1.00.

### 4.2 Threshold Logic
The final decision is conservatively thresholded:
- **LONG:** `score > 0.20` AND `confidence > 0.45`
- **SHORT:** `score < -0.20` AND `confidence > 0.45`
- **WAIT:** Otherwise.

### 4.3 Confidence Penalties
Voter confidence is not merely the sum of signal confidences. It is mathematically penalized by:
- **Elevated Volatility:** Subtracts 0.15 from base confidence if the Volatility signal indicates an elevated state.
- **High Entropy:** Modulates confidence based on permutation and spectral entropy measurements.

## 5. The ML Advisor (DEC-019)

The Decision Engine integrates with the Learning System exclusively via an optional `ml_advisor` payload. The ML Advisor is strictly bounded—it is an **advisor**, not a dictator.

### 5.1 Bounded Confidence Bonus
If the ML Advisor strongly agrees with the Voter's directional decision:
- A bonus is added to the Voter's confidence.
- **Cap:** The bonus is strictly bounded at $\leq 0.15$.
- **Formula:** `min(0.15, 0.30 * (ml_agreement - 0.5))`
- If the directions mismatch, the bonus is exactly 0.0.

### 5.2 Dissent Warning System
If the Learning System violently disagrees with the deterministic Voter, it triggers a `ml_dissent` warning:
- **Fires when:** Voter confidence $\geq 0.45$, ML probability is strongly opposed ($>0.60$ or $<0.40$), and ML model agreement is $\geq 0.60$.
- **Action:** Generates a warning string. The underlying decision is **never changed**, ensuring the deterministic engine retains ultimate authority.

## 6. The Recommender System

The `recommend()` function is the top-level orchestration entrypoint. It composes the entire lifecycle:
1. Calls `compute_all_signals()` to snapshot L1-L4 features.
2. Learns or loads weights (via `learn_weights` or regime-conditional loading).
3. Executes `vote()` aggregation.
4. Assesses risk via `assess_risk()` and `position_sizing()`.
5. Locates K-NN equivalents via `find_analogues()`.
6. Generates explanations via `explain_recommendation()`.

### 6.1 Regime-Conditional Wiring (v0.13.0)
If `use_regime_conditional_weights=True`, the recommender dynamically partitions historical signal accuracy based on the L3 `market_regime.regime.active_state_code`. It selects the specific rolling Logistic Regression weight vector trained *exclusively* on history that matches the current regime code.

## 7. The Weight Learner

Instead of opaque neural networks, signal weights can be dynamically fitted using a rolling Logistic Regression over the last `K` bars (`window=200`).

### 7.1 Interpretable Mechanism
- **Features:** The $N \times 22$ matrix of historical signal votes (direction $\times$ strength).
- **Target:** The sign of the $H$-bar forward log-return.
- **Weights:** The normalized absolute coefficients ($|\beta_i|$) of the Logistic Regression model.
- **Interpretability:** This ensures that if the Trend signal stops working, its $\beta$ approaches 0, and its weight smoothly decays, fully transparently.

## 8. Historical Analogues (K-NN)

The Analogues system contextualizes the current market by finding the $K=10$ most mathematically identical historical periods.
- **Schema v1:** 19-dimensional space, heavy on L1/L2 features.
- **Schema v2:** High-dimensional L4 embedding space (`L4_EMBEDDING_IDS`) combined with 5 curated L2/L3 numerics.
- **Metric:** Euclidean distance on Z-scored features.
- **Output:** Reports the median forward return, best/worst case, and hit rate of these exact historical matches.

## 9. Risk Sizing & Assessment

Position sizing is entirely derived from immutable Atlas volatility and statistical features.

### 9.1 Core Metrics
- **ATR%:** Average True Range over 14/30 bars normalized to price.
- **VaR (5%):** Value at Risk of historical returns.
- **Expected Shortfall:** Conditional VaR at the 5% tail.
- **Ulcer Index:** Deep drawdown duration penalty.
- **Hurst Index:** Determines mean-reverting ($H < 0.5$) vs trending ($H > 0.5$) stability.

### 9.2 The Hard Cap
The `position_sizing()` function calculates size as:
`Base Size = Directional Strength × Confidence × Volatility Dampener`
**Strict Enforcement:** The final size is hard-capped at **2.5%** of capital. It dynamically shrinks during high ATR periods.

## 10. The Explainer System

The explainer is a deterministic, template-based natural language generator.
- **NO LLMs are used.** It cannot hallucinate.
- It iterates through the evidence array attached to every signal.
- It partitions arguments into `bullish`, `bearish`, and `neutral` buckets.
- It yields a `beginner` summary paragraph and a dense `pro` bulleted list detailing the exact arithmetic behind the score.
