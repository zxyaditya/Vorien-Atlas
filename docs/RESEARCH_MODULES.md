# Atlas Research Modules Architecture

## 1. Executive Summary

The Atlas infrastructure houses five proprietary, institutional-grade quantitative research modules. These modules utilize advanced statistical, econometric, and machine-learning techniques to exploit specific market inefficiencies. They operate autonomously as background daemons, feeding signals or executing logic continuously.

These modules include:
1. **Stat Arb (Statistical Arbitrage)**
2. **Whale Hunter (Microstructure & On-Chain)**
3. **Funding Arb (Derivatives Rate Arbitrage)**
4. **Futures Core**
5. **Options Core**

## 2. Stat Arb Module

The Statistical Arbitrage module identifies and exploits mean-reverting relationships between co-moving asset pairs or baskets. It is driven by rigorous econometric testing rather than simple correlation.

### 2.1 Cointegration Testing
- **Engle-Granger Two-Step Method:** Used for initial rapid screening of pairs to identify stationary residuals.
- **VECM (Vector Error Correction Model):** Applied to multivariate baskets to identify the long-term equilibrium rate and the short-term adjustment dynamics.

### 2.2 Copula Models
To capture non-linear and tail dependencies that Pearson correlation misses, the module models the joint cumulative distribution function (CDF) using various Copula families (e.g., Clayton for left-tail dependence, Gumbel for right-tail).

### 2.3 Regime Detection (HMM)
Hidden Markov Models (HMM) classify the spread's behavior into discrete latent states (e.g., "Trending", "Mean-Reverting", "Chaotic"). The strategy only trades when the HMM detects a high-probability "Mean-Reverting" state.

### 2.4 Spread Estimation & Execution
- **Kalman Filter:** Dynamically updates the hedge ratio ($\beta$) between assets, allowing the model to adapt to structural breaks without rigid lookback windows.
- **Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) Process:** The residual spread is modeled as an OU process to calculate the exact half-life of mean reversion.
- **stat_arb_daemon:** A background worker running continuously (`apps/api/stat_arb_daemon.py`). It polls the latest L1 price data, updates the Kalman filter states, evaluates the OU thresholds, and dispatches trades if the z-score exceeds critical bounds.

## 3. Whale Hunter Module

The Whale Hunter module operates at the intersection of Layer 1 microstructure data and on-chain analytics. It detects institutional accumulation and distribution phases in real-time.

### 3.1 Market Microstructure Analysis
- Evaluates highly granular tick-level metrics, such as tick-size clustering, iceberg order detection, and time-weighted average price (TWAP) footprinting.
- Analyzes wick geometries (`microstructure.geometry.wick_difference_ratio`) to detect aggressive limit-order absorption.

### 3.2 On-Chain Data Integration
- Tracks major wallet movements, exchange inflows/outflows, and stablecoin minting.
- Maps on-chain anomalies to centralized exchange orderbook behavior.

### 3.3 Order Flow Imbalance (OFI) Detection
- Computes `microstructure.expansion.execution_imbalance_20`.
- OFI tracks the net pressure of market orders aggressively lifting the offer or hitting the bid. Extreme imbalances preceding structural breaks serve as the primary trigger for the Whale Hunter.

### 3.4 whale_hunter_daemon
Runs continuously to ingest Level 2 orderbook snapshots and trades. It calculates volume-weighted bid-ask spreads and flags transient liquidity vacuums where a "whale" has swept the book.

## 4. Funding Arb Module

The Funding Arb module is a market-neutral yield generation system. It capitalizes on the persistent premium (or discount) between perpetual futures contracts and spot markets.

### 4.1 Arbitrage Mechanics
- When the funding rate is positive (Longs pay Shorts), the module shorts the perpetual swap and goes long on the equivalent spot asset.
- When the funding rate is negative, the reverse is executed.
- The position is delta-neutral, purely harvesting the funding payments.

### 4.2 Monitored Instruments
- Constantly scans high-liquidity pairs across Binance, Bybit, and OKX.
- Filters out illiquid pairs to prevent slippage from destroying the yield.
- Normalizes annualized yield across 1h, 4h, and 8h funding epochs.

### 4.3 Execution Logic
- Utilizes TWAP/VWAP execution to enter the basis trade without moving the market.
- Monitors the borrow rate of the spot asset to ensure net yield remains positive.
- Automatically unwinds if the basis converges prematurely or crosses the liquidation safety threshold.

## 5. Futures Core

The Futures Core module (`atlas_futures_core` and `derivatives_registry.py`) encapsulates the logic required to model, predict, and trade leveraged perpetual futures.

### 5.1 Features and State Machines (69 Dimensions)
- Orderbook depth gradients, microprice-midpoint divergence, Level-1/5/10 imbalance ratios.
- Volume-Synchronized Probability of Toxicity (VPIN 50 & 100), Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD), aggressor buy ratio.
- Perpetual funding rates, funding velocity ($d\text{Rate}/dt$), funding acceleration, 50-period Z-score, basis spread.
- Open interest (OI) velocity, liquidations cascades, and long/short ratio skew.
- Margin accounting states: Cross vs. Isolated tracking.

### 5.2 futures_paper_daemon
- An autonomous daemon (`apps/api/futures_paper_daemon.py`) running continuously on 5-minute candle alignment.
- Pulls live public Binance Futures orderbooks and trades.
- Computes all 69 features in real-time, runs inference with `futures_xgb_v1.pkl`, and manages a live paper portfolio.
- Enforces VPIN circuit breakers (rejecting toxic entry $> 0.70$, emergency close $> 0.85$).
- Exposes telemetry via `GET /api/futures/paper-state`, execution logs via `GET /api/futures/logs`, and model specifications via `GET /api/futures/model-spec`.

### 5.3 Dedicated Web Workspace
- Visualized in the web terminal at `/futures` (Futures & Perpetuals Terminal) with real-time carry/squeeze dynamics, live CVD taker ratio, and execution logs console.

## 6. Options Core

The Options Core module (`atlas_options_core` and `derivatives_registry.py`) brings institutional derivatives volatility surface analytics and Greeks computation to Atlas.

### 6.1 Greeks & Surface Analytics (45 Dimensions)
- First-order Greeks: Delta, Vega, Theta, Rho.
- Higher-order Greeks: Gamma, Vanna ($d\Delta/d\sigma$), Volga ($d\nu/d\sigma$), Charm ($d\Delta/dt$), Color, Speed.
- Volatility surface dynamics: ATM IV (30d/60d/90d), 25-Delta Risk Reversal (skew), 25-Delta Butterfly (smile curvature / fat tails), Term Structure slope (Contango/Backwardation).
- Dealer market structure: Net Dealer Gamma Exposure (GEX in $M), Net Dealer Vega Exposure (VEX), Gamma Flip Level, Max Pain Strike, Volatility Risk Premium (VRP = IV - RV).

### 6.2 options_paper_daemon
- An autonomous daemon (`apps/api/options_paper_daemon.py`) that continuously monitors live Deribit options orderbooks and Binance underlying spot data.
- Dual-ML classifier architecture: Spot Direction Classifier (54.8% Acc) and Volatility Expansion Classifier (56.2% Acc).
- Executes delta-neutral volatility arbitrage with dynamic VPIN vega sizing and $2.0\times \text{ATR}$ adaptive trailing stops.
- Exposes portfolio state via `GET /api/options/portfolio`, logs via `GET /api/options/logs`, and model spec via `GET /api/options/model-spec`.

- Visualized in the web terminal at `/options` (Options Vol Surface & Greeks) with live Deribit options positions, Black-Scholes Greeks surface, and smile curvature
